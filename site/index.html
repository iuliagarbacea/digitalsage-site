About

A small firm, on purpose.

Digital Sage was founded in Bucharest in 2022. We work with enterprise and public-sector clients in Belgium and for EU institutions, mostly on long engagements where knowing the process matters more than the tool: ERP, procurement, order management, financial processes, and the testing that proves they work.

We keep the firm small so that the person who scopes the work is the person who does it.

  • Written firstEvery decision ends up in a specification that someone can build from and test against.
  • TraceableRequirements, tests and changes link back to the business need that caused them.
  • DirectNo account managers, no hand-offs. You talk to the person doing the work.
  • CertifiedInternationally certified in test analysis, business-process modelling and cloud AI. Certificates available on request.
